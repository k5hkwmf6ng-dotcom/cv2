Dear HelloFresh Design Team,

I make complex systems simple to move through, and I am always curious about how new AI tools can help teams move faster and decide better. My career has been about taking dense, tangled information and turning it into something people can navigate with confidence. That is the work the Consumer UX team is doing across discovery, personalization, shopping and subscription management, where the difficulty is rarely in adding another feature, it is in making a large system feel simple and considered at every step.

At Nacos Marine, I led end-to-end design and product strategy for vessel-monitoring products across web, desktop, and onboard HMI, shipping to 13,000+ vessels and 30,000+ daily users. My process is human-centered and evidence-led. I draw out the non-obvious needs through co-creation workshops and user research, synthesise what I find into a narrow, well-defined problem, and communicate a clear design rationale grounded in research, so a cross-functional team of product managers, engineers, and developers across several countries can align. I hold that bar down to the last decision about hierarchy and layout: on one product that work cut time-to-task by 70% on pages carrying 70+ live data points, and a customer who saw the release came back and ordered a second interface. Earlier, at Cytiva, I contributed to a design system and internal tools, including an hour-tracking redesign that cut daily task time for 500+ employees.

What excites me most is a question that sits underneath any personalized product: where should a system act on its own, and where does the person stay in control? I co-designed an AI storytelling system for children, letting the child set the direction at each turn while the model generates within guardrails. During my master's in Human-Centered AI, I researched and designed a mobile reading app for low-vision users, working from rough concepts to a tested design to understand how a system should step in without breaking a user's focus. I use AI inside the work as well, with Figma Make, Claude Design and Claude Code, to synthesise research, prototype an idea the same day it comes up, and close the gap between a design and working code.

HelloFresh is also one of the few places where that craft meets a problem I have already had my hands on. At DTU I helped build an Android app to cut household food waste and presented it to investors at the Green Challenge. I have always chased new contexts to learn from, from exchange studies in Tokyo and Lisbon to picking up filmmaking, where I shoot, colour, and edit short documentaries from my travels. I would be glad to bring that curiosity and craft to the Consumer UX team.

Warmly,
