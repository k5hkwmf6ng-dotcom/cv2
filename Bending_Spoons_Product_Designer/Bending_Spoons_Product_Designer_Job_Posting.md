## Role, as reconstructed

Help shape outstanding user experiences and impact the success of the products by creating flawless
UI and UX designs, from conceptualization to delivery — a human-first approach to designing
products that delight users by being as effective as they are aesthetically pleasing.

- Work closely with product managers and software engineers to propose new features and improve
  the quality of both mobile and desktop apps.
- Focus on user testing and research so that customers are always at the core of product designs;
  validate findings with hard data.
