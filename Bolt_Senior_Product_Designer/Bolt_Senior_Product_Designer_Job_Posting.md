# Bolt — Senior Product Designer, Identity and Verification

**Company:** Bolt (bolt.eu). Mobility platform: ride-hailing, food delivery, scooters, e-bikes,
car-sharing. 200M+ customers, 4.5M driver and courier partners, 50 countries, €1bn+ funding,
Sequoia-backed.
**Team framing:** small, autonomous teams; startup ownership at global scale.
**Working model:** hybrid, at least 12 in-office days per month.
**Accent used:** `#32BB78` (Bolt Jungle Green, per Brandfetch).

Posting text supplied by the candidate.

---

## The role, in their words

Identify meaningful user and business opportunities within Bolt's identity and verification flows,
and turn them into simple, consistent, and scalable product experiences. Partner closely with
product and engineering to optimize acquisition funnels using data-informed design, while
establishing unified best practices that balance usability, security, and regulatory requirements
across apps and regions.

**Responsibilities:** quickly prototype and iterate design concepts; confidently present design
ideas to senior stakeholders using a balance of data-driven insights and strong design intuition;
collaborate with engineers, product managers, and researchers; advocate for user-centric,
high-quality design while balancing multiple tasks and timelines; push visionary solutions that
challenge existing ideas, set the quality bar, be an exemplary designer to peers; contribute to
evolving design systems and consistency in UI/UX patterns.

**About you:** collaborative, design-led thinker who speaks the language of product and influences
stakeholders at all levels; extensive experience as a Senior Product Designer with a portfolio
showing exceptional UX craftsmanship; knows when to prioritize fast iterations and prototypes;
fluent English, pitches design vision through storytelling backed by user research and design
intuition; proficient in Figma, FigJam, Jira, Confluence; passionate about mentoring designers.

**Also stated:** "using AI daily is a baseline expectation for everyone… You're still responsible
for the output, so the standard stays high." And: "Experience is great, but what we really look for
is drive, intelligence, and integrity. So even if you don't tick every box, please consider
applying!"

## Gaps — what the CV cannot claim

1. **Identity, verification, KYC.** No experience anywhere in the source material. The nearest
   honest adjacency is designing inside hard technical and accessibility constraints, which is how
   the profile frames it.
2. **Acquisition funnels, conversion, retention.** Bolt asks for funnel optimisation. No funnel or
   conversion metric exists. `A/B testing & experimentation` is listed as a skill with no result
   attached. If a number exists, it belongs in Nacos bullet 2.
3. **Mentoring designers.** The posting asks for it twice. Source has "maintained a design system
   with other designers" and "coordinated the design team" for the AGH conference (**400+
   attendees**), which is the only leadership-of-designers signal and sits in Education.
4. **Seniority.** They ask for "extensive experience as a Senior Product Designer". Title on the CV
   is UX Designer, five years. The posting explicitly invites applications that do not tick every
   box.
5. **Jira and Confluence.** Named in the posting, absent from the tool list. Add if she uses them,
   which is likely.
6. **Multi-region / multi-app.** "Across apps and regions" is close to the cross-country
   collaboration bullet but not the same thing; not claimed.
