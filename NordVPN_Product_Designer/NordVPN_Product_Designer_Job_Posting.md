# Product Designer — NordVPN Apps (Nord Security)

## Posting (verbatim summary)

The Design Team is a multidisciplinary group of Product designers, Web designers, UX
researchers, UX writers and Graphic designers, building across NordVPN, NordPass, Saily
and Coveron. The role sits in the **NordVPN Apps Design team**: owning the design for your
squad, running projects end to end, and increasingly questioning the brief, not just
delivering on it.

### Main responsibilities

- **Own the whole flow.** Design experiences that feel native on iOS, Android, macOS,
  Windows and browser extensions while staying recognizably Nord. Explore multiple
  directions, think through problems from the ground up, always know the *why*.
- **Push the problem, not just the pixels.** Surface assumptions early with your PO and
  engineers, challenge briefs when the evidence points elsewhere, sometimes make the call
  that the best design is removing a flow entirely.
- **Craft with the system.** Apply the design system without losing intent and help it
  grow. When you spot a gap, propose the component, not the workaround.
- **Ship, don't just design.** Back decisions with evidence, partner with UX Research or
  run validation yourself, stay close to engineers through the build, own Design QA.

### Core requirements

- 3+ years in product design (mobile & desktop apps), fluency across platforms, growing
  ownership of complex problems.
- Smart prototyping — right tool for the question; quick mockups for flow logic,
  high-fidelity prototypes when interaction needs to feel real, AI for breadth.
  Prototype with real data.
- Ownership and communication — handle disagreement constructively, bring engineering
  realities in before they become blockers.
- Attention to detail — meticulous eye for visual and interaction detail.

### Additional (strong plus)

- Accessible and inclusive interfaces; applying **WCAG** conformance criteria in design.
- **Motion design.**
